## Phase Gates (apply to every phase)

- **Entry**: prior phase exit met OR phase scoped as parallel; build/lint/test green; demo personas seeded.
- **Exit**: 100% of P0 cases Pass; ≥95% of P1 cases Pass; no Open critical/high defects in scope; defects logged with reproducible steps.
- **Evidence**: screenshot or short clip per failing case; console + network HARs for security/perf cases.
- **Severity rule**: P0 (blocker), P1 (must-fix), P2 (should-fix), P3 (nice).
- **Personas**: Anonymous, Admin, Platform Owner, Manager, Employee, Accountant.
- **Animal types**: All, Poultry, Cow.
- **Viewports**: Desktop (1440), Tablet (768), Mobile (360); landscape variants where noted.
